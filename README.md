# 🌀 The Arc Spinner
### *Tactile physics, zero distractions.*

[![Live Demo](https://img.shields.io/badge/🚀_Launch_App-Live_Preview-a855f7?style=for-the-badge&logo=rocket&logoColor=white)](https://prasannaone.github.io/The-Arc-Spinner/)

<br>

<p align="center">
  A minimalist, high-fidelity PWA widget capturing the fluid motion, haptics, and physical ergonomics of the iconic Arc search spinner.
</p>

</div>

---

## ⚡ Highlights

* **Pure Physics Engine** — Grab and toss the widget across your screen with organic rubber-band spring physics, or flick the background arena for smooth in-place momentum.
* **Kinetic Depth** — Asymmetric layered blades with realistic drop shadows and custom gradient sheens.
* **Immersive Feedback** — Crisp micro-vibration haptics and synthesized mechanical click audio.
* **Adaptive Aesthetics** — Seamless light/dark mode switching paired with curated neon color palettes.
* **Native PWA Ready** — Install directly to your mobile home screen with full offline caching and setting persistence.

---

## 🕹️ Gestures & Controls

| Action | Gesture |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Move & Toss** | Touch & drag directly on the spinner widget |
| **Spin in Place** | Swipe or drag across the empty background space |
| **Toggle Zero-G** | Triple-tap the header title dot |

---

## 🛠️ Built With

* **Vanilla JavaScript** — High-performance `requestAnimationFrame` physics loop.
* **CSS Custom Properties** — Dynamic theming and GPU-accelerated transforms.
* **Service Workers & PWA** — Fast offline caching and native app behavior.
